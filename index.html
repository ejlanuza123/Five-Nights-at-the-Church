THREAT: HIGH

Behavior: Freezes under camera observation. Check on her regularly.

DEFENSE ACTION: When she reaches your Left Door, press DOOR LIGHT [Z] to catch her silhouette peering into the doorway, then immediately press LOCK DOOR [Q] until she retreats!

THE HALOED SAINT

THREAT: CRITICAL
The Martyr • Haloed Stalker of the Cloister Route: CAM 03 (Confessional) → Window Glass → CAM 05 (Outside Door) → Left Door

Behavior: Stalks through the front hallway outside your observation window before flanking to the left door.

DEFENSE ACTION: When he stands outside the front glass, press WINDOW SPOTLIGHT [W]! The intense beam forces him to retreat. If he slips around to CAM 05 outside your door, prepare to lock the Left Door [Q]!

THE VEILED MOURNER

THREAT: MODERATE

Behavior: Crawls low inside the HVAC return duct. Listen for metallic clanging.

DEFENSE ACTION: When she peeks out of the office vent opening, press VENT LIGHT [R] and immediately press SEAL VENT [E] to slam the steel fire shutter down!

04 POWER MANAGEMENT & BATTERY PRESERVATION

1
Do not leave doors locked continuously:

The magnetic door lock drains power at a heavy rate every second it is sealed. Lock only when statues are actively at your doorway threshold!

2
Use lights in short pulses:

Spotlight and downlights consume battery rapidly. Tap keys [Z], [W], and [R] briefly to confirm threats rather than leaving them on.

3
Flip down CCTV monitor between camera sweeps:

Lower the digital monitor with [SPACE] when not scanning rooms to minimize baseline electrical drain.

4
Total Blackout = Instant Vulnerability:

If battery reaches 0%, all lights and motorized locks immediately disengage, leaving your office exposed in total darkness!
